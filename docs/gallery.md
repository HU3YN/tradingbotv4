# A tour of Terrarium

[Back to the README](../README.md) · [Setup and trading instructions](../README.md#your-first-paper-trading-session)

Fresh captures of the current application, taken October 24, 2026 (Pacific time). These show an unconnected paper workspace with completed historical research. Blank P&L and empty positions are intentional: no account profits, trades, or credentials were invented for the screenshots. The resting poses were staged through the existing animation controls in the browser for documentation; application source and trading state were not changed.

## The habitat

The main screen brings together the market connection, session totals, four residents, their proposed strategy assignments, recent activity, and morning-schedule shortcut. Its plants, branches, full leaf floor, and water droplets are part of the live 3D scene.

![Full desktop dashboard and all four residents](screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Strategy lab

Run the historical tournament here, then review the finalist table, benchmark comparison, and equity curves. The report download contains the full candidate and elimination results. The pictured returns belong to the historical research run; they are not paper-account profits.

![Strategy lab with historical finalists and equity curves](screenshots/strategy-lab.png)

## Trading journal

The journal records controller events and confirmed fills. This workspace has no connected account, so it shows the honest empty state. It will fill as paper trading runs.

The speaker button plays or pauses. **Music** opens the track selector and volume slider. Soft jazz and quiet piano are included locally and loop without a streaming connection. The old wind-noise loop is gone.
